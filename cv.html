Professional Summary

Fellowship-trained Radiation Oncologist with more than 10 years of international oncology experience across Chile and Australia. Currently practising in a busy regional public and private oncology environment in Queensland, managing a broad range of tumour streams including breast, prostate, gastrointestinal, genitourinary, skin, thoracic and palliative malignancies. Experienced in contemporary radiotherapy techniques including IMRT, VMAT, SRS and SABR, with strong interests in stereotactic radiotherapy, quality improvement, research, and evidence-based patient-centred care.
