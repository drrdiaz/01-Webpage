Posted: 4 June 2026

GE HealthCare announced FDA 510(k) clearance for MIM Contour ProtegeAI+ 2.0, an AI-enabled auto-contouring tool for radiation therapy planning. Narrative review examines AI for prognosis and adaptive treatment monitoring

Posted: 2 June 2026

A Cureus review surveyed AI use in prognostic modelling, adaptive monitoring, response assessment, and toxicity prediction. Its main value is not hype but restraint: the paper repeatedly points back to inconsistent methods, limited external validation, and the need for governance before routine use.

This arXiv preprint describes a workflow-integrated large-language-model system for physician summaries and potential trial identification. It is interesting as an operations signal, but it remains early-stage evidence rather than proof of clinical accuracy or downstream patient benefit.

npj Digital Medicine outlines a human-centric model for intelligent treatment planning

Published: 10 January 2026

A perspective in npj Digital Medicine argues for human-centric intelligent treatment planning, with AI operating under clinician oversight rather than replacing planning judgment. It is useful because it focuses on workflow design, safety, and planner interaction instead of treating automation as a goal by itself.

Frontiers study reports daily AI-assisted adaptive bladder radiotherapy workflow

Published: 12 February 2026

A Frontiers in Oncology paper reported daily AI-assisted adaptive small-margin radiotherapy during trimodal therapy for muscle-invasive bladder cancer. This is closer to practice-facing evidence than a generic AI claim, because it sits inside an actual adaptive workflow rather than a purely technical benchmark.

Research

Explainable machine learning study targets xerostomia prediction during head-and-neck radiotherapy

Published: 23 April 2026

A Radiation Oncology paper used longitudinal delta-radiomics and explainable machine learning to predict radiation-induced xerostomia during head-and-neck treatment. It matters because the model is not only predictive but interpretable, which is much more clinically useful than a black-box score alone.

Editorial Note

This vault mixes vendor announcements, peer-reviewed articles, and preprints. That is intentional, but they should not be treated as equal evidence. Announcements show direction, papers show stronger evidence, and preprints should be read with the most caution.
